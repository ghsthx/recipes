    Notes

    Let the first blin be a test — pan temperature and thinness always take one try to dial in. For a toddler portion, cut into strips and skip anything more than a light dusting of powdered sugar or plain jam. Leftover bliny keep well wrapped for 2 days; reheat briefly in a dry pan.
